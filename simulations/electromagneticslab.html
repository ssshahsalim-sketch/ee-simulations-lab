Coulomb's Law Formula:

Force is directly proportional to charge products and inversely proportional to distance squared ($r^2$).

Electric Field Intensity ($\mathbf{E}$)

The Electric Field is a property of the source charge configuration alone at a given point in space.

$$\mathbf{E} = \lim_{q_t \to 0} \frac{\mathbf{F}}{q_t} \quad \text{[V/m or N/C]}$$

Electrostatic Force ($\mathbf{F}$)

Force experienced depends on both the source field and the test charge ($q_t$) placed at that location.

$$\mathbf{F} = q_t \mathbf{E} \quad \text{[N]}$$
