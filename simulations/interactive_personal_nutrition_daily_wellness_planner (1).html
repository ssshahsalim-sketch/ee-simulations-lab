Understanding Iron & Hemoglobin

The essential physiological pathway for tissue oxygenation and physical energy.

1. Dietary Iron Heme & Non-Heme Sources
2. Hemoglobin Oxygen-binding protein in RBCs
3. Oxygen Transport Delivered to muscles & brain
4. Cellular Energy ATP production & stamina
Vegetarian (Non-Heme)

Lentils, chickpeas, beans, spinach, tofu, sesame & pumpkin seeds. Requires Vitamin C to enhance absorption.

Non-Vegetarian (Heme)

Eggs, fish, chicken, and meats. Higher baseline bioavailability (15-35%).

Absorption Timing Rules

Enhance: Add lemon/amla (Vit C).
Inhibit: Avoid tea/coffee within 1 hr of iron meals.

Important Safety Standard: Do not start self-prescribed high-dose iron supplements without laboratory confirmation (Serum Ferritin / Hemoglobin) and medical oversight. Excessive iron accumulation can cause tissue damage.
