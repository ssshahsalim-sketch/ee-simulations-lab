X-Ray Tube Assembly

High-Voltage Radiation Source

The X-ray tube contains an evacuated glass/metal envelope housing a tungsten filament cathode and a rotating tungsten anode target. High voltage accelerates electrons across the vacuum gap.

Key Physics: Bremsstrahlung emission

Why Do Bones Look White on X-Ray?

1. High Photoelectric Absorption

Bone contains high atomic number calcium (Z=20). The probability of photoelectric absorption scales roughly with Z³. Bone absorbs far more photons than muscle or fat.

2. Inverse Grayscale Mapping

Fewer X-ray photons reach the detector behind bone. Detectors register low radiation dose. Digital processing maps low exposure to light/white and high exposure (air) to dark/black.

Radiation Protection & ALARA Principle

ALARA stands for As Low As Reasonably Achievable. The three cardinal rules of radiation safety are:

1. TIME Minimize duration of exposure
2. DISTANCE Maximize distance (Inverse Square Law)
3. SHIELDING Use lead aprons & walls
