Faraday's Law of Induction

A changing magnetic flux through a coil induces an EMF. The faster the flux changes — either by spinning the magnet faster or by opening more turns of wire to it — the larger the induced voltage:

EMF = −N · dΦ/dt

N is the number of turns; the minus sign (Lenz's Law) says the induced current always opposes the change that created it.

Flux Through A Rotating-Field Coil

With the magnet's axis at angle θ to the coil's normal, and field B threading a coil of area A:

Φ(t) = N · B · A · cos(θ) EMF(t) = N · B · A · ω · sin(θ)

where ω = dθ/dt is the magnet's angular speed. This is exactly why a spinning magnet produces an alternating (sinusoidal) EMF — the classic AC generator.

What Each Control Changes

  • Faucet flow sets the target angular speed ω the water wheel drives the magnet toward
  • Turns (N) and loop size (A) both scale the EMF linearly / by area — more turns or a bigger loop, more voltage for the same spin
  • Barn doors mechanically mask the field reaching the coil, scaling effective B — fully closed ≈ no induction even while the magnet keeps spinning

Reading The Bulb & Voltmeter

The voltmeter needle tracks instantaneous EMF directly. The bulb's brightness tracks power, which goes as EMF², so it flickers twice as fast as the voltage waveform and dims much faster than the voltage does as speed drops.

P ∝ EMF²

Simulation Units Note

Field strength, loop area, and the resulting EMF/voltmeter numbers here are simulation-scaled for a readable, responsive display — not calibrated against a specific real magnet or coil. The relationships (linear in N, ω, and A; quadratic power law) are the physically correct part to take away.

Why This Matters In The Field

  • Same principle behind every rotating AC alternator/generator — hydro, wind, diesel-genset, and utility turbo-alternators all induce EMF this way
  • Barn-door-style flux control is the visual analogue of field weakening / excitation control used to regulate generator output voltage
  • Coil turns vs. loop area trade-off mirrors real winding design choices between voltage rating and physical size
