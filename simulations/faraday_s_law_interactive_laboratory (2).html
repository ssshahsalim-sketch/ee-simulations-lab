Faraday's Law Equation
$\mathcal{E} = - N \frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$

The induced electromotive force ($\mathcal{E}$) is directly proportional to the number of wire loops ($N$) and the rate of change of magnetic flux ($d\Phi_B / dt$). Moving the magnet faster or adding turns creates a stronger EMF.
