Strategy #1

Time Allocation & Rounding Strategy

Solve GATE in 2 passes. Pass 1: Attempt all 1-mark questions and easy 2-mark questions (aim for 50-60 marks in first 100 mins). Pass 2: Revisit 2-mark NATs and marked questions.

Strategy #2

Handling NAT (Numerical Answer Type)

NATs carry NO negative marking! Never leave a NAT unattempted. Double-check decimal precision requested (e.g., up to 2 decimal places) before entering.

Strategy #3

Virtual Calculator Efficiency

Master bracket operations and memory functions [MC, MR, MS, M+] in the GATE scientific calculator to save 15–20 seconds per complex AC power or load flow calculation.

Strategy #4

Working Professional Consistency

A steady 3 hours on weekdays + 5 hours on weekends produces over 800+ quality study hours by Feb 2027. Focus on PYQ pattern repetition over exhaustive textbook reading.
