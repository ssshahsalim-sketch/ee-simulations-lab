Hydrogen Protons (Spin Mechanics)

Hydrogen nuclei (single protons) act like tiny magnetic dipole moments. B₀ aligns them; RF pulses tip their magnetization vector away from z-axis into transverse plane (x-y).

MRI Hardware & Signal Processing Flow

1. Superconducting Magnet: Generates B0 (1.5T/3.0T) inside cryostat cooled by liquid Helium (4 Kelvin).
2. RF Transmitter: Synthesizes high-power RF excitation pulses tuned precisely to Larmor Frequency.
3. Gradient Amplifiers & Coils: Modify B0 linearly across X, Y, Z axes for spatial position encoding.
4. Receive Coils & LNA: Detect tiny microvolt Precession FID signals and amplify with Low Noise Amplifiers.
5. ADC & DSP Computer: Digitizes raw signal, fills 2D K-Space matrix, executes 2D Inverse Fast Fourier Transform.

MRI Safety & Ferromagnetic Hazard Demonstration
