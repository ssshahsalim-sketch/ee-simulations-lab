Key Panel Hierarchy

  1. HT Metering & VCB panel (11kV incoming, discom boundary)
  2. Distribution Transformer(s) 11kV/433V, Dyn11 vector group typical
  3. Main LT Panel (MLTP) — ACB incomer, bus coupler if 2 transformers, outgoing ACBs/MCCBs to each riser
  4. DG Synchronizing/ATS panel — auto changeover for essential bus
  5. Riser Bus Duct / Armoured cable — vertical rising main per shaft
  6. Floor Distribution Board (FDB) — one per floor per riser type, MCCB/MCB incomer with metering
  7. Flat/Tenant DB — individual meter + RCCB + MCBs (light/plug/AC/geyser sub-circuits)

Contractor Workflow Checklist (High-Rise, 20–50 Floors)

  1. Load list & connected/demand load calculation → sanction load application to discom
  2. HT/LT single line diagram, substation layout, transformer & DG sizing → approval from Electrical Inspectorate
  3. Cable/busduct riser sizing with fault-level and voltage-drop calculations
  4. Earthing & lightning protection design (IS 3043, IS/IEC 62305)
  5. Fire NOC electrical compliance — fire pump feeder, FRLS/fire-survival cabling, emergency lighting, PA system power
  6. Floor-wise DB schedule, cable schedule, point wiring drawings
  7. Panel manufacturing (type-tested per IEC 61439), factory testing (routine tests)
  8. Site installation, cable laying, termination, megger/insulation resistance testing
  9. Earthing resistance testing, protective relay setting & commissioning
  10. Third-party inspection (Electrical Inspector), energisation, and handover with as-built drawings & O&M manual
