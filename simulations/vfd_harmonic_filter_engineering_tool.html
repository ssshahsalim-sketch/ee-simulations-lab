🔌 Physics of Non-Linear Diode Conduction

A standard 6-pulse VFD utilizes a 3-phase diode bridge rectifier feeding a DC-link capacitor. The diodes only conduct when line voltage exceeds the DC bus capacitor voltage, creating non-sinusoidal pulsating currents.

DC Bus & Diode Parameters

Higher capacitance = narrower, sharper current pulses = higher THD!
Line reactors widen the conduction angle and reduce current spikes.
Characteristic Harmonic Equation:
h = 6k ± 1   (where k = 1, 2, 3...)

Characteristic orders for 6-pulse:
5th, 7th, 11th, 13th, 17th, 19th, 23rd, 25th...

🛡️ Single-Tuned Passive Filter & Resonance Simulation

A single-tuned passive filter creates a low-impedance path to ground at its resonant frequency $f_r = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$, trapping dominant harmonic currents. However, it creates parallel resonance with the grid upstream!

Filter Parameters

Normally tuned slightly below 5th (e.g. 4.7th) to prevent over-loading.
⚠️ Parallel Resonance Risk

Parallel resonance occurs at 185 Hz (3.7th). Ensure this point is far from 5th or 7th harmonics!

Tuned Notch (Series Resonance) = Low Impedance Peak (Parallel Resonance) = High Impedance amplification!

⚡ Active Harmonic Filter (AHF) Anti-Phase Injection

An AHF monitors non-linear load current in real-time, extracts harmonic distortion components via FFT/DSP, and drives IGBTs to inject exact anti-phase current ($I_{comp} = -I_{harmonic}$), canceling harmonics at the PCC.

6-Stage AHF Control Logic Sequence
1. Current CT Sensor
2. DSP & FFT Extraction
3. Anti-Phase Calc
4. PWM Modulation
5. IGBT Injection
6. Sinusoidal Grid
Step 1: Current Transformers (CT) measure the distorted non-linear VFD current waveform ($I_{load}$).
