Signalling System Logic Explanation

Signals maintain safe headway. A GREEN signal means at least 2 blocks ahead are clear. A YELLOW signal warns that the next block is clear but the following block is occupied. A RED signal mandates a complete stop before the block boundary to prevent collisions.

Key Railway Signalling Concepts Quick Reference

Fixed Block Signalling Tracks are partitioned into static blocks. Only one train is permitted per block to maintain safe braking distance.
Fail-Safe Interlocking Arrangement of signal apparatus that prevents conflicting movements through switches and tracks.
ATP (Automatic Train Protection) Safety system that continuously checks train speed against signal limits and applies brakes automatically if violated.
ETCS / CBTC Systems Modern wireless moving-block technology where signal aspect data is transmitted continuously directly to driver displays.
