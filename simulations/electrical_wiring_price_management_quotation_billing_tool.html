How to Use ElectroWire Pro

Typical Electrician Workflow:

  1. Create Project & Customer: Click "New Project", enter customer name, site location and project type.
  2. Room-wise Wiring Points: Go into the project details, add wiring points grouped by room (e.g., Living Room -> 2 Fans, 4 Lights, 1 AC point).
  3. Materials & Equipment: Add wires, switches, MCBs and select whether contractor supplies them or customer purchases them directly.
  4. Additional Work & Transport: Add wall chasing, DB dressing, earthing or delivery charges.
  5. Generate Quotation: Click "Generate Quotation" to create an official document with itemized rates, terms, and signature lines.
  6. Record Advances & Invoices: Convert approved quotations to final invoices and record customer payments (Cash/UPI).
  7. Track Cost & Profit: View actual worker wages and material purchase costs to calculate exact net profit margin %.
