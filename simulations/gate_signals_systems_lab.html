⚡ GATE Key Insight
Continuous sinewaves $\sin(\omega t)$ are ALWAYS periodic for any real $\omega$. However, discrete sinewaves $\sin(\omega n)$ are periodic ONLY IF $\frac{\omega}{2\pi}$ is a rational number $\frac{k}{N}$.

⚡ Energy & Power Signal Analyzer
Analytical Equations
Energy: $$E = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} |x(t)|^2 dt$$ Power: $$P = \lim_{T \to \infty} \frac{1}{2T} \int_{-T}^{T} |x(t)|^2 dt$$
Signal Classification Report

Power P: 0 Watts

Rule of Thumb: If $0 < E < \infty \implies P = 0$ (Energy Signal). If $0 < P < \infty \implies E = \infty$ (Power Signal). An unstable signal usually has $E=\infty, P=\infty$.

🔀 Continuous-Time Convolution Interactive Simulator
Core GATE Topic
$$y(t) = x(t) * h(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x(\tau) h(t - \tau) d\tau$$
1. Fold: $h(\tau) \to h(-\tau)$
2. Shift: $h(t-\tau)$ by $t$
3. Multiply: $x(\tau) \cdot h(t-\tau)$
4. Integrate: Compute Area
〰️ Continuous-Time Fourier Series (Harmonic Synthesizer)
$$x(t) = \frac{4A}{\pi} \sum_{n=1,3,5\dots}^{N} \frac{1}{n} \sin(n \omega_0 t)$$
Gibbs Phenomenon: At points of discontinuity, partial sums of Fourier series overshoot the actual signal value by approximately 9% regardless of how large $N$ becomes!

💡 GATE Quick Tricks & Common Traps

⚡ GATE Shortcuts & Tricks

  • Convolution Duration: If $x(t)$ has duration $T_1$ and $h(t)$ has duration $T_2$, $y(t) = x(t)*h(t)$ has total duration $T_1 + T_2$.
  • Area Property of Convolution: Area of $y(t)$ equals (Area of $x(t)$) $\times$ (Area of $h(t)$).
  • Nyquist Rate of Product: For $z(t) = x_1(t) \cdot x_2(t)$, maximum frequency component $f_{max} = f_1 + f_2 \implies \text{Rate} = 2(f_1 + f_2)$.

⚠️ Common GATE Traps

  • Trap 1: Confusing $x(2t - 4)$ with $x(2(t-4))$. Scaling applies ONLY to the variable $t$.
  • Trap 2: Assuming discrete sinewaves are always periodic. Check if $\frac{\omega}{2\pi} \in \mathbb{Q}$.
  • Trap 3: Forgetting that ROC MUST NOT contain any poles in Laplace / Z-transforms.

⭐ Continuous Fourier Transform Pairs
$$\delta(t) \xleftrightarrow{\mathcal{F}} 1$$ $$u(t) \xleftrightarrow{\mathcal{F}} \pi \delta(\omega) + \frac{1}{j\omega}$$ $$e^{-at}u(t) \xleftrightarrow{\mathcal{F}} \frac{1}{a + j\omega}$$ $$\text{rect}(t/T) \xleftrightarrow{\mathcal{F}} T \text{sinc}\left(\frac{\omega T}{2\pi}\right)$$
⭐ Laplace & Z-Transforms
$$\mathcal{L}\{e^{-at}u(t)\} = \frac{1}{s+a}, \quad \text{Re}(s) > -a$$ $$\mathcal{Z}\{a^n u[n]\} = \frac{z}{z-a} = \frac{1}{1 - a z^{-1}}, \quad |z| > |a|$$
