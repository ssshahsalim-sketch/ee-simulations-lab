🔑 KIRK SD Series Interlock — E/W Configuration Simulator

Animated, interactive reproduction of the five standard SD-Series trapped-key configurations from the KIRK Interlock Instruction Manual (IB/B-T/06, page 2): 1 Cylinder (E), 2 Cylinders (EE), 2 Cylinders (EW), 3 Cylinders (EWW), and 3 Cylinders (EEW). Insert keys, then operate the shared bolt to watch it extend or withdraw and see exactly which keys become trapped or released — per the manual's own rule.
