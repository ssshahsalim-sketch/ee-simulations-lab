GATE Formula Sheet & Quick Shortcuts

High-priority formulas and quick problem-solving techniques for GATE Control Systems.

Second-Order System Formulas

  • Damped Frequency: $\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}$
  • Peak Time: $T_p = \frac{\pi}{\omega_d}$
  • Max Peak Overshoot: $\%M_p = e^{-\frac{\pi \zeta}{\sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}}} \times 100\%$
  • Settling Time (2% tolerance): $T_s \approx \frac{4}{\zeta \omega_n}$

GATE Quick Tricks

  • Routh Array Shortcut: If any coefficient of the characteristic equation is zero or negative, system is NOT stable!
  • Bode Initial Slope: Initial magnitude slope is $-20N$ dB/dec, where $N$ is system type.
  • Root Locus Asymptote Centroid: $\sigma_A = \frac{\sum \text{Re}(\text{Poles}) - \sum \text{Re}(\text{Zeros})}{P - Z}$
