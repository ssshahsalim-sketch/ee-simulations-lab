Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Resistor Color Code Calculator ("the App") is developed by Salim Shah / EE Simulations Lab. This page explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function. All calculations happen entirely on your device.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, storage, or any other device permissions beyond what is required to run as a standard Android application.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via the developer contact listed on the Google Play Store listing for this app.
